# Toolix Brand

- Product: Toolix (toolix.dev)
- Audience: Power users, students, remote workers, developers
- Voice: Focused, fast, restrained
- Constraint: Sub-second load, 0 CLS, client-side execution

## Color System (Impeccable)

- Background: Slate-tinted white (`#F8F7F5`) / Obsidian dark (`#121214`)
- Accent: Electric Blue (`#2563EB`)
- Text: Near-black (`#0F1115`) / Soft white (`#F4F3F0`)
- Neutrals: Warm slate (`#7A7670`)
- NO pure black (#000), NO pure white (#FFF), NO purple gradients

## Typography

- Primary: Geist Sans / Inter Display
- Tight negative tracking on headings
- NO generic system stacks or Arial
